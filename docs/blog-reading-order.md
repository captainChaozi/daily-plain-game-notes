# Plain Game guides: five posts and a reading order

Source: [Plain Game Guides & Tips](https://plaingame.fun/blog) · Play: [Plain Game daily estimation game](https://plaingame.fun/)

The Plain Game blog has five guides, all published on 23 Oct 2026. They overlap a little, so this is the order I would read them in.

| # | Guide | Read it if… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Looking for Games Like Plain? Try Plain Game](https://plaingame.fun/blog/games-like-plain) | You came from the original Plain and want to know what is different |
| 2 | [Why Being Twice as Big and Half as Small Score the Same](https://plaingame.fun/blog/estimation-scoring-ratios-explained) | Your scores feel random; ratio scoring explains 10%, 50% and 5× misses |
| 3 | [How to Estimate Years and Dates in Plain Game](https://plaingame.fun/blog/estimating-years-and-dates) | You lose points on year rounds, which are scored by years away, not ratio |
| 4 | [How to Get Better at Estimation Games](https://plaingame.fun/blog/how-to-improve-estimation) | You want a repeatable seven-round routine with units and reference points |
| 5 | [How to Think Fast in 30-Second Estimation Rounds](https://plaingame.fun/blog/timed-rounds-strategy) | You run out of time; three free seconds, then four metres of altitude per second |

## One idea from each

1. Plain Game adds untimed Discovery and unlimited practice on top of the daily flight.
2. A guess twice as big and one half as small score the same.
3. A decade guess still earns something on a year round.
4. Read the unit first, then anchor on one value you know.
5. Spending the full 30 seconds costs only about 11 points of altitude; a better answer can be worth far more.
