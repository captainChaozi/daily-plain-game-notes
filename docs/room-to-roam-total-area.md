# Room to roam: total area, not land area (Daily Plain Game)

Game: [Daily Plain Game](https://plaingame.fun/) · Scopes: [Data sources & limitations](https://plaingame.fun/sources) · Collections: [Field guide](https://plaingame.fun/field-guide)

## The collection

"Room to roam" has 60 country entries measured in km², from Russia (17,098,246) to Belgium (30,528). Figures are frozen and rounded.

## The scope that trips people

The sources page lists the scope as **total surface area, not land area alone**. Total area includes inland water. For countries with large lakes or wetlands, total and land area can differ noticeably, and a reference you remember may be the other number.

## A related collection

"The long road" also uses total area (land plus water) in rounded km². Keep both in the same mental bucket.

## How to estimate

1. Place the target in a band first: under 100,000 km², around 500,000 km², millions.
2. Pick a candidate you are confident sits near that band.
3. Scoring is by ratio, so being 1.5x off is better than 2x off even if the absolute gap looks bigger.

The bank has not received an exhaustive independent audit; boundaries and measurement dates can differ between sources.
