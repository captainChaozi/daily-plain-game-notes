# Scoring notes

How points work in the [Daily Plain Game](https://plaingame.fun/), summarised from the site's scoring page and blog.

- A flight has **seven rounds**: five name-the-answer rounds near a target value and two numeric estimates.
- Each timed round lasts **30 seconds**. Closer answers earn more points; each point lifts the plane ten metres. After three seconds the plane gently descends.
- **Bullseye** answers earn a bonus.
- **One hidden round doubles** your points.
- Most rounds are scored by how close your value is **as a ratio** of the target; see [scoring ratios explained](https://plaingame.fun/blog/estimation-scoring-ratios-explained).
- **Year rounds** are different: base score = round(100 × exp(−years_off / 16)), so ten years off still earns 54. See [estimating years and dates](https://plaingame.fun/blog/estimating-years-and-dates).
- A timed-out round earns nothing.
